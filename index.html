About Mia

Telling Utah's Stories, One Frame at a Time

media.by_mia is a Utah-based photography studio specializing in portrait, wedding, and lifestyle imagery. From the red rock of Moab to the snow-capped peaks of the Wasatch Range, every session is shaped by the landscape and the people in front of the lens.

Every shoot is collaborative — we'll talk through your vision, choose the perfect Utah backdrop, and create images you'll treasure for a lifetime.
